# 🌟 MAK2+ Community

Welcome to the MAK2+ community! We're building an open-source, community-driven qPCR analysis tool based on mechanistic modeling.

## 🎯 Our Vision

MAK2+ is developing as a **nonprofit cooperative** where:
- 🔓 **Free access** for all researchers and educators
- 🗳️ **Democratic governance** by community members
- 💡 **Open science** principles guide development
- 🤝 **Sustainable funding** through member contributions

## 🤝 How to Contribute

We welcome contributions from everyone!

### For Researchers
- 📊 **Share your data**: Help us validate on diverse qPCR datasets
- 📝 **Cite us**: Include MAK2+ in your publications (citation coming soon)
- 💬 **Give feedback**: Tell us what features you need

### For Developers
- 🐛 **Fix bugs**: Check our [Issues](https://github.com/gboggy2/MAK2-plus/issues)
- ✨ **Add features**: Propose new analysis methods
- 📚 **Improve docs**: Make MAK2+ easier to use

### For Educators
- 📖 **Create tutorials**: Help others learn mechanistic qPCR modeling
- 🎓 **Use in teaching**: Introduce students to computational biology
- 🔬 **Share examples**: Contribute educational datasets

## 💰 Support MAK2+

MAK2+ is currently free and will remain free for basic use. We're exploring a **cooperative membership model**:

### Proposed Tiers (Coming Soon)
- **Free Tier**: Full access to all features, community support
- **Member Tier** ($5-20/month): Priority compute, voting rights, advanced features
- **Contributor Tier**: Free member benefits for code contributors, educators, grant writers

All membership fees will support:
- 🖥️ Infrastructure and hosting costs
- 👨‍💻 Developer time for new features
- 📊 Validation studies and benchmarking
- 📚 Documentation and tutorials

## 📊 Current Status

- ✅ Core MAK2+ model with primer depletion
- ✅ 3-tier optimization (Fixed, LHS, Differential Evolution)
- ✅ Replicate analysis and dilution series
- ✅ Ct vs D0 precision comparison
- ✅ Global threshold calculation
- 🚧 Bayesian uncertainty quantification (in development)
- 🚧 Multi-target analysis (planned)
- 🚧 Publication-ready export (planned)
